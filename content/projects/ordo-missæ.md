Ordo Missæ is a digital guide to the Order of Holy Mass in the Roman Rite, presenting the liturgy in Chinese, English, and Latin. Bringing these texts together in one place, it helps readers follow the celebration across languages and become more familiar with the prayers, responses, and sequence of the Mass.

The project offers a practical reference for personal preparation and following along during the liturgy. Its multilingual presentation also makes it easier to encounter the traditional Latin text alongside its English and Chinese counterparts.
