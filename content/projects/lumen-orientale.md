Lumen Orientale is an editorial website dedicated to the Christian East and its spiritual heritage. Its name, meaning “Eastern light,” frames the project around the traditions and spiritual life of Eastern Christianity. The site gives this subject a dedicated online home, with an editorial format suited to publishing and organizing written material for readers interested in Christian heritage.

The site is built with **Hugo**, a static site generator that turns source content and templates into pages that can be served without a traditional application server. It uses the **Blowfish** theme as its design and layout foundation, and is deployed on **Cloudflare Pages**. Together, this setup provides a lightweight publishing workflow for a content-focused website.
