My personal website is the central home for my work as a freelance writer, social media content creator, lay acolyte, and liturgical master of ceremonies. Its focus is the stories, people, and sacred traditions of the Catholic Church, told through writing, interviews, filming, video editing, and digital content.

The site organizes that work into several parts: a biography and curriculum vitae introduce my background; the portfolio highlights work with Catholic events and communities; the gallery collects visual material; and the blog provides space for articles and reflections. Calls to explore the work, read the blog, or get in touch give visitors clear next steps, including a contact form for writing, journalism, digital content, and documentary enquiries.

The site is built with **Next.js**, using **JavaScript** and **TypeScript**. This stack supports a component-based site structure and typed application code across the pages and interactive elements.
