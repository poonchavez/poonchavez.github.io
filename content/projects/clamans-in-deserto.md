Clamans in Deserto (荒野中的呼喊者, “a voice crying in the wilderness”) is a Chinese-language website for faith-centered writing and reflection. Its title evokes the biblical image of a voice preparing the way, giving the site a clear spiritual frame for its published material. The project offers a dedicated place to share and read those reflections in Chinese.

The site is built with **Hugo**, a static site generator that converts content and templates into prebuilt web pages. The **Blowfish** theme provides its layout and visual foundation, while **Cloudflare Pages** hosts the published site. This static publishing setup is well suited to a content-led site whose core experience is reading articles and reflections.
