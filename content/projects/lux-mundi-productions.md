Lux Mundi Productions is a website design project for a media production brand. The site creates a dedicated digital home for the company, giving visitors a first introduction to its identity and creative work.

The design is intended to give the brand a clear and memorable presence while keeping the experience focused on the work. A considered visual hierarchy and straightforward browsing help visitors understand who the company is and where to explore further. The result is a polished starting point for presenting Lux Mundi Productions online and growing its presence over time.

The project brings the brand and its work together in one place, with the website serving as both an introduction for new visitors and a central destination to share. It is designed to support the company’s identity as it develops and to make the work easy to discover.
