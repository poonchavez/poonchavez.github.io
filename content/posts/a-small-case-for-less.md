There’s a particular kind of confidence in leaving something out. Not because the thing wasn’t considered, but because it was—and it didn’t need to stay.

We add because adding feels like progress. Another feature, another line, another layer of explanation. Sometimes it is progress. Often, it’s just the comfortable feeling of doing something visible.

## Subtraction takes a point of view

Removing a thing means deciding what matters most. It asks us to trust the useful signal inside all the noise. That’s harder than piling on options, but it makes space for people to find their way through.

When a page feels confusing, I try not to ask what it needs next. I ask what it could do without. The answer is rarely dramatic. A sentence. A button. One more clever idea.

The quiet version tends to last longer.
