A blank canvas is full of possibility, which is another way of saying it’s full of decisions you haven’t made yet. I find it hard to think clearly in that much open space.

A rough draft gives the conversation somewhere to go. It can be wrong in useful ways. It lets us point, react, and discover the version we couldn’t have described beforehand.

## Progress needs a shape

The first sketch doesn’t need to be good. It needs to exist. A few words, a clumsy diagram, a working link. Anything concrete enough to help someone say, “more like this” or “not quite that.”

Make something small. Put it in front of someone. Pay attention to what happens next.
